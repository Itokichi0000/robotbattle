## Observations (2026-10-28, mvp-2)

- **Symmetry**: RAIJIN/FUJIN win shares are within sampling noise (n=200/cell). Exact symmetry is proven by the mirrored-input unit test; the simulator itself breaks same-millisecond ties in favour of RAIJIN, which is negligible with random timestamps.
- **Pacing**: with ≤10 players/team matches run the full 270s (KO 0–40%). With 30–100 players/team, KO is common but happens at the end (avg end 265–267s, i.e. mostly the final clash, which the spec allows: 「最後の衝突でHPが0になればKO」). Under SPEC_BASELINE the same scenarios ended at 212–250s with 85–97% KO.
- **Dominance**: SPEC_BASELINE had pure attack averaging 87% vs other strategies. mvp-2 reduces it to 75%, and a thoughtful mixed strategy ("adaptive": reactive defence, finish contests, take neutrals, charge near specials/late) is roughly even with it (44–57%). Attacking remains the strongest single verb — acceptable for a 5-minute party game, but revisit after human playtests.
- **Team size**: one extra player (10 vs 11) moves win rate to ~30:70. Mitigations in place: automatic balancing, live display of players/AP per team. Underdog compensation is a future option (default OFF, D-017).
- **Open questions for playtests**: do humans actually defend reactively? Is 3 AP per entry enough engagement? Should clashes scale damage with participant count?
